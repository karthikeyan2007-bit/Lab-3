About the book

Santiago, a young shepherd from Spain, dreams of a treasure hidden near the Egyptian pyramids. His journey across the desert brings him mentors, love and loss, and teaches him to listen to his heart and follow his "Personal Legend".

Key themes

  • Following dreams
  • Destiny
  • Self-discovery
