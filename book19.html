About the book

Twins Estha and Rahel grow up in Kerala in 1969, where small events lead to a family tragedy. Told in rich, playful language, it looks at love, caste and the rules about who may love whom.

Key themes

  • Family
  • Caste
  • Forbidden love
  • Memory
