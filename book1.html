About the book

Winston Smith lives in Oceania, a state ruled by the Party and its ever-watching leader, Big Brother. He works rewriting history to match the Party's version of the truth, but quietly begins to question it — and to imagine a life of his own.

Key themes

  • Surveillance
  • Totalitarianism
  • Truth & propaganda
  • Freedom
