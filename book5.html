About the book

Dr. Kalam traces his life from a modest childhood in Rameswaram to leading India's space and missile programmes. It is a story of hard work, mentors and teamwork, written to inspire young Indians to dream big.

Key themes

  • Perseverance
  • Science & nation-building
  • Leadership
  • Inspiration
