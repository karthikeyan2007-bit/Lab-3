About the book

In a small Alabama town in the 1930s, young Scout Finch watches her father, lawyer Atticus Finch, defend a Black man falsely accused of a crime. Through a child's eyes, the story explores prejudice, courage and growing up.

Key themes

  • Justice
  • Racial inequality
  • Moral courage
  • Childhood
